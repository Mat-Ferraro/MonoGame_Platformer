L02 flat lighting regression fixture correction
=============================================
Test file: PlatformerSandbox.Tests/Presentation/Lighting/LFlatLightingDiagnosticLevelTests.cs
The L02 flat test intentionally makes Vines.png an FG3 Silhouette that receives
lighting from the green light, so the original assertion ReceivesLight=false
was stale. The corrected test verifies that the vines receive light, do not
receive shadows, belong to FG3 Silhouette, and exactly one authored light
targets FG3. The independent Unlit.png no-light/no-shadow checks are retained.
No production source or LDtk content is changed.
