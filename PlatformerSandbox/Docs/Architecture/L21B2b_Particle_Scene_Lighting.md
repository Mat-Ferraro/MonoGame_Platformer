# L21B2b — World particle scene lighting

World-space effect parts default to `Lighting: Scene`, regardless of their visual draw layer or blend mode. The gameplay layer is the lighting receiver for these effects; its shadow and light routing policy is inherited. Screen-space/UI particles remain unlit. An authored part may explicitly opt out using `"lighting": "Unlit"`. This is not an emitter; existing particle light candidates are unchanged.

The existing layered GPU compositor captures premultiplied particle artwork, multiplies its RGB by ambient plus per-light shadowed illumination, and composites it at the original draw boundary. Alpha particles and additive particles are captured separately. The latter add already alpha-weighted RGB once, preserving destination alpha. Neither particles nor their textures become occluders. Empty/offscreen lit passes are skipped. No new textures, shader, simulation changes or per-particle render targets. Alpha passes precede additive passes; unlit parts draw after lit parts of the same blend. A maximum of six extra layer passes may occur (three visual layers times two blends); map reuse and timing are future profiling work. The existing RGBA8 albedo capture can clamp overlapping additive particle energy before HDR composition; a floating-point capture is a possible follow-up for extremely bright effects. An explicitly unlit gameplay layer retains the old direct path. Particle playground and non-layered gyms remain unchanged because they have no scene lighting context.

## Acceptance — EMISSIVE LIGHTING LAB

Start in preset 3, with two colored sources and no emission/bloom. Jump and land near cyan, magenta, between lights and inside each wall shadow. Dust and chips must inherit the appropriate colored light, and a single particle can straddle a shadow boundary. Verify additive specks do not create dark rectangles. Press 1 (ambient only), 4 (all effects), 5 (dark walls); compare with lighting disabled, and check UI particles remain unaffected. Check moving shadows and soft shadow settings in Lighting Integration Test. Verify FPS and lighting submit times with heavy particle effects. No dedicated GPU runtime was available to execute these acceptance checks.
