# HK02 — Manual lighting coverage and selection diagnostics

## Reproduced cause
The HK01 Quality Lab has three repeated showcase sections. Each section has two active authored LightSource entities in Showcase mode and three surface-generated emissive proxies (cyan, pink, small animated surface). The other authored shape variants are filtered by the workload. The existing High budget admits at most 12 logical lights but only five shadow owners. The planner prioritizes authored `level` shadow lights, then others by priority and ordinal ID. A shadow-dependent `emissive` or `flash` light without a shadow slot is dropped entirely to prevent light leaking through walls. Sprite emission is separate, so a source can still glow.

Using the frozen HK01 authored inventory at the reported camera centers (71.51, 15.04) and (78.17, 15.04), 720p at 32 px/world unit, the conservative viewport candidates increase from seven to eight. The third section's pink emitter enters the candidate set, sorts before the center-section pink emitter, and takes its shadow slot. The center emitter is still in range. This is reproducible in the source-derived Python check and covered by a real C# presenter regression test. No GPU runtime validation was available here.

## Implementation
- New manual `Light Coverage` selector, default `Preserve Scene Lights`, independent of graphics presets and optimization toggles. Uses up to 12 logical lights and 12 shadow owners (existing production material logical-light ceiling), with the selected preset's normal sample and particle budgets. Both planner and all GPU layer consumers receive the same budget. Existing shadow budget policy, including deliberate shadowless Low, is available as `Quality Budget`.
- Preserve mode applies only to the interactive Quality Lab Normal fixture outside Stress. Automated benchmark/visual runs, the Engine Performance Lab, special fixtures, Stress, and normal gameplay keep their previous budgets. No source assets or shaders are changed.
- `Lights Active / Visible / Shadowed` and dropped counts are actual planner dispositions, not pixel visibility. The Light Inspection selector exposes each world light's current position and exact admission reason, including workload filtering, outside-view culling, logical-light limit, shadow limit, and active-with/without-shadow states. The visible count is after tag filtering and uses the planner's conservative influence bound; actual receiver contribution may still be zero from attenuation, routing or occlusion. Stress proxy counts are summarized, not listed as scene lights.
- Preserve mode may cost more FPS and can still hit the 12-light cap at larger viewports or future denser scenes. Source-derived coverage checks bound the current fixture at 10 candidates for 720p and 12 for 1080p; 1440p can exceed this cap. The UI reports drops rather than implying unlimited coverage. Budget-related popping in ordinary gameplay remains a separate future production-selection policy task; we intentionally did not silently raise production budgets or alter the benchmark reference policy.

2. Launch Release/detached. Choose Lighting Quality Lab, Normal, Showcase, Ultra, Preserve Scene Lights. Freeze scene/wall animation for the initial check. Walk right and back across the middle section boundary, or use Horizontal Sweep. The center pink emitter should remain active/shadowed while its influence overlaps the view. Inspect it (X near 70.6) under Light Inspection; confirm no budget drops at 720p. Repeat with animation on, other presets and optimization flags on/off.
3. Toggle Quality Budget to deliberately observe original budget-dependent behavior. Return to Preserve Scene Lights for correctness review. Do not compare Preserve-mode FPS directly with the original benchmark.
4. Optional `py .\Tools\po_run_benchmarks.py --po10f --tier quick` verifies the historical benchmark path still runs, but is not a substitute for the wide-lab check.
